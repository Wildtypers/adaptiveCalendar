Technical Challenges
Purpose

The purpose of risk management is to identify technical and project-related risks that may affect the successful development of Predictive Planner. By identifying these risks early, mitigation strategies can be incorporated into the development process to reduce their impact and improve the likelihood of delivering a functional, scalable, and maintainable application.

Technical Risks
Learning Flutter

As the primary development framework, Flutter represents the largest learning curve of the project. Since the application is being built while simultaneously learning Flutter, development speed may initially be slower and architectural decisions may require refinement as experience increases.

Impact: High
Probability: High

State Management and Analytics

Implementing Riverpod and designing the analytics dashboard require understanding application state, data flow, and synchronization across multiple screens. Incorrect implementation could lead to inconsistent application behavior and increased maintenance complexity.

Impact: Medium
Probability: Medium

Database Design

The database schema must support both the MVP and future AI-driven features. Poor schema design could require significant restructuring when cloud synchronization, machine learning, or automatic scheduling are introduced.

Impact: High
Probability: Medium

Prediction Algorithm

The adaptive prediction system is the core differentiator of Predictive Planner. Developing an algorithm that produces useful predictions while limited historical data exists presents a technical challenge during the MVP phase.

Impact: Medium
Probability: Medium

Learning Multiple Technologies Simultaneously

The project requires learning Flutter, Dart, Riverpod, Drift, Clean Architecture, and modern software engineering practices concurrently. This increases project complexity and may extend development time.

Impact: High
Probability: High

Project Risks
Time Constraints

The project combines learning new technologies with building a portfolio-quality application. Balancing feature development, testing, documentation, and software engineering best practices within the planned schedule presents the greatest overall project risk.

Impact: Very High
Probability: High

Scope Creep

As additional ideas emerge throughout development, there is a risk of expanding the MVP beyond its intended scope. Adding advanced functionality too early could delay completion of the core adaptive scheduling features.

Impact: Medium
Probability: Medium

Business Risks
User Adoption

Predictive Planner ultimately aims to become a startup. The primary business risk is investing significant development effort into a product that fails to attract or retain users. Success depends on delivering a meaningful productivity improvement over existing calendar applications.

Impact: High
Probability: Medium

Risk Priority
Priority	Risk
1 (Highest)	Running out of development time
2	Database schema design
3	Machine learning implementation
4	Learning Flutter
5	Scope creep
6	Prediction accuracy
7 (Lowest)	User interface complexity
